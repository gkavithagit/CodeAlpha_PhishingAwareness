Phishing Awareness Quiz

Project

CodeAlpha Phishing Awareness Training

Objective

Evaluate the reader's ability to identify potential phishing attempts and select appropriate security responses.

Instructions: Choose the best answer for each question. Check the answer key after completing all questions.

---

Question 1: Suspicious Email

You receive an unexpected email claiming that your account will be suspended unless you click a link immediately. What should you do?

A. Click the link immediately.

B. Reply with your password.

C. Verify the request through an independently trusted channel.

D. Forward the email to everyone.

Question 2: HTTPS Security

Which statement about HTTPS is correct?

A. Every HTTPS website is legitimate.

B. HTTPS encrypts the connection but does not guarantee that a website is trustworthy.

C. HTTPS prevents all phishing attacks.

D. HTTPS guarantees that a website belongs to a bank.

Question 3: Smishing

What does smishing mean?

A. Phishing through SMS or text messages.

B. A secure password storage technique.

C. A network monitoring tool.

D. A type of firewall.

Question 4: Suspicious Attachment

You receive an unexpected attachment from an unfamiliar sender. What is the safest action?

A. Open it immediately.

B. Disable your antivirus and open it.

C. Verify the sender and the reason for the attachment before opening it.

D. Send your login credentials to the sender.

Question 5: Password Exposure

You accidentally enter your password on a website that you suspect is fraudulent. What should you do?

A. Ignore the incident.

B. Change your password through the legitimate service, contact support if appropriate, and take additional account-protection steps.

C. Share the password with a friend.

D. Visit the suspicious website again.

Question 6: Sender Verification

What is a good way to check an unexpected email requesting sensitive information?

A. Trust it because it uses a company logo.

B. Check the sender and verify the request through an independent, trusted channel.

C. Trust it because the email looks professional.

D. Reply with your personal details.

Question 7: Social Engineering

Which technique is commonly used in phishing attacks?

A. Creating urgency or fear.

B. Automatically improving computer performance.

C. Increasing internet speed.

D. Compressing files.

Question 8: Unexpected Payment Request

You receive an unexpected message from someone claiming to be your manager and requesting an urgent payment. What should you do?

A. Transfer the money immediately.

B. Verify the request using a separate, trusted communication channel and follow your organization's payment procedures.

C. Send your bank details.

D. Ignore all company security procedures.

Question 9: Multifactor Authentication

How does multifactor authentication help protect an account?

A. It removes the need for passwords in every situation.

B. It adds another verification step, making account access harder with only a stolen password.

C. It guarantees that phishing can never succeed.

D. It automatically removes malware.

Question 10: Reporting Phishing

What should you do when you identify a suspicious email at college or work?

A. Forward it to everyone.

B. Click every link to investigate.

C. Report it through the appropriate IT or security reporting channel.

D. Reply to the sender with your password.

---

Answer Key and Explanations

1. C — Independently verify unexpected requests before taking action.
2. B — HTTPS encrypts the connection but does not establish that the site itself is legitimate.
3. A — Smishing is phishing delivered through SMS or text messages.
4. C — Unexpected attachments should be verified before opening.
5. B — Secure the legitimate account promptly and take appropriate recovery steps.
6. B — Sender details and requests should be verified independently.
7. A — Attackers often exploit urgency, fear, trust, or curiosity.
8. B — Payment requests should be verified separately and follow established procedures.
9. B — Multifactor authentication adds a verification layer, although it cannot prevent every attack.
10. C — Reporting helps the appropriate security team investigate and respond.

Conclusion

Phishing awareness helps individuals recognize suspicious messages, verify unexpected requests, protect sensitive information, and report potential attacks.

Ethical note: This quiz is intended for cybersecurity education and does not involve real phishing attacks or the collection of personal information.
