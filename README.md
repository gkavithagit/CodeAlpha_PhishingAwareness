📌 Project Overview

Phishing Awareness Training is a cybersecurity awareness project developed as part of my CodeAlpha Cybersecurity Internship.

Phishing is a social engineering technique in which attackers attempt to deceive people into revealing sensitive information, clicking malicious links, opening unsafe attachments, or performing unauthorized actions.

The primary goal of this project is to educate users about common phishing techniques, help them recognize suspicious communications, and encourage safer online behavior.

The project combines an educational presentation, a fictional phishing email analysis, an awareness quiz, and supporting documentation.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Understand phishing attacks and their impact on individuals and organizations.
- Identify common types of phishing and social engineering techniques.
- Recognize warning signs in suspicious emails and messages.
- Understand the importance of verifying senders, links, and unexpected requests.
- Learn appropriate responses to suspected phishing attempts.
- Promote cybersecurity awareness and responsible digital behavior.

📚 Project Contents

1. Phishing Awareness Presentation

An educational presentation introducing phishing attacks, common attack methods, warning signs, social engineering, and prevention techniques.

Topics covered:

- Introduction to phishing.
- How phishing attacks work.
- Types of phishing: email phishing, spear phishing, smishing, vishing, and clone phishing.
- Common warning signs in suspicious messages.
- Social engineering tactics such as urgency, fear, trust, and curiosity.
- Safe practices for avoiding phishing attacks.

📂 File: ""presentation/phishing_awareness.pdf"" (presentation/phishing_awareness.pdf)

2. Phishing Email Analysis Case Study

A fictional email scenario used to demonstrate how suspicious messages can be examined for phishing indicators.

Analysis includes:

- Urgent or threatening language.
- Unexpected account verification requests.
- Suspicious or unverified senders.
- Potentially unsafe links.
- Recommended actions for responding to suspected phishing.

The case study emphasizes verifying unexpected requests independently rather than clicking links or providing sensitive information.

📂 File: ""examples/phishing_email_analysis.md"" (examples/phishing_email_analysis.md)

3. Phishing Awareness Quiz

A knowledge-check activity designed to reinforce the concepts covered in the presentation.

The quiz covers phishing indicators, social engineering, safe responses, multi-factor authentication, and reporting suspicious messages.

📂 File: ""quiz/phishing_quiz.md"" (quiz/phishing_quiz.md)
